01

Design

Simple and elegant as a piano

Its extensive face with vertical, proportionate lines gives a comfortable and desirable form. Thanks to the latest technology, the switch and the socket have the same size — a more pleasing visual effect.

02

Multi-gang design

Up to 5 gangs, many combinations

i7 switches and sockets share multi-gang frames of up to 5 gangs. The 76-type mounting box is applied; for multi-gang frames a dedicated Simon mounting box keeps the design perfectly aligned.

  • Frames1–5 gang
  • Box76 type
03

Safe and soft push

The J-shape toggle

The feeling is comfortable and easy: thanks to the J-shape toggle system the angular movement is very precise — only 4.5° — so every press is faster and safer.

  • ToggleJ-shape
  • Angle4.5°
04

Installation

Flexible wire, easy to connect

The saddle-shaped terminal has a wider hole that fits high-section wire to meet the power needs of the family, without damaging the wire. The children safety lock rejects any plug pushed in by abnormal objects.

  • Saddle terminal
  • Child safety lock
05

Simple and sophisticated

Base and frame, one design

The base and the frame are integrated in the same design and made from PC and steel — high strength and no deformation. The painted steel base is insulated and anti-corrosion.

  • MaterialPC + steel
  • Anti-corrosion
06

Exquisite colours

A matt, techno colour effect

The matt and techno colour effect adapts to any interior style and gives a sophisticated feeling, on switches, sockets and frames alike.

  • White
  • Champagne
  • Mocha
  • Dark Grey
  • Black Doff
07

Energy saving

A new electronic technique

i7 achieves a two-wire system — suitable for incandescent, fluorescent, halogen and LED with only two wires — saving energy and reducing carbon.

  • Touch time delay
  • Presence detector
  • Voice and light
08

Charging & connectivity

Type A + Type C USB outlet

A convenient way to charge all your devices. The wall-mounted Wi-Fi AP router gives wired and wireless connection at the same time, saves space for cables and, installed in several rooms, covers the whole home.

  • USB A + C
  • Wireless AP router
  • 13A BS switched socket
09

Home & hospitality

Entertainment and hotel solutions

HDMI + USB, VGA-3.5 and AV-S multimedia outlets with TV, data and telephone sockets for home and commercial use — plus shaver outlets, keycard switches and doorbell switches with "Do Not Disturb" and "Make Up Room" indicators.

  • Multimedia
  • Keycard
  • Doorbell · DND / MUR
10

i7 Simon Smart

Practical and simple, just as you imagine

A conventional switch capable of turning your house into a smart home: light on/off, tunable white and dimming, curtain control, AC control and scene setting — with a gateway and wireless remote scene switches.

  • App control
  • Voice · Google, Alexa, Siri
  • Wall switch
  • Automation
11

Simon ELV

RCU / KNX switches

The complete i7 range now also comes with RCU and KNX extra-low-voltage switches — the open wired smart control system for homes, hotels and commercial developments.
